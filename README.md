# MechForge AI

## Intelligent Design-for-Manufacturing Platform

MechForge AI is an engineering intelligence platform for:

- Design-for-Manufacturing analysis
- Mechanical drawing intelligence
- CAD geometry inspection
- Manufacturing process recommendations
- Cost and machining-time estimation
- Design optimization
- Engineering reports
- AI engineering assistance
- Supplier and quotation management

### Current Release

**v3.1.0 — Professional Engineering Workspace**

### Core Workspaces

- Dashboard
- New Analysis
- CAD Studio
- AI Assistant
- Suppliers
- Reports & History
- Settings

### Supported CAD

- STEP / STP
- STL

### Important

MechForge AI provides engineering decision support.
Manufacturing decisions, dimensions, tolerances and safety-critical requirements
must be verified by a qualified engineer.
